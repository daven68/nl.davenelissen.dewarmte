Monitor your DeWarmte heat pump or heat pump water heater with Homey. The available data is tailored to the connected model.

For Pomp AO, the app shows operating status, supply, return and target temperatures, electrical power, heat input and output, water flow, thermostat demand, gas boiler assistance and general fault status.

For Pomp T, the app shows operating status, top and bottom boiler temperatures, electrical power, heat input and output and general fault status.

Use Flow to respond to status changes. Measurements are available in Homey Insights. The dashboard widget brings the most important measurements and statuses together at a glance and adapts to the connected model.

The app is completely read-only and has been tested with DeWarmte Pomp AO and Pomp T. Other DeWarmte models have not yet been tested. The app will be expanded step by step with more energy insights, Flow options and additional functionality.
